## 6. Key Business Insights  

1. **Product Concentration:** Over **86 %** of revenue comes from the Laptop and Mobile categories.  
2. **Profitability Gap:** Low‑margin items (Table, Chair) dilute overall profit margin.  
3. **Regional Imbalance:** The South region lags significantly; growth opportunities exist there.  
4. **Declining Trend:** Both revenue and profit have fallen each month, despite a relatively stable profit margin (≈10 %).  
5. **Order Frequency:** Only 10 orders in three months; the business relies on high‑value transactions rather than volume.

---

## 7. Business Recommendations  

| Area | Recommendation | Rationale |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| **Product Portfolio** | **Focus marketing and inventory on Laptop and Mobile**, while reviewing pricing or cost structure for Table and Chair. | These two products generate >86 % of revenue and ~81 % of profit. |
| **Margin Improvement** | **Introduce a modest price increase or cost‑reduction initiative** for low‑margin items (Table, Chair) to lift overall profit margin. | Their profit contribution is disproportionately low relative to revenue. |
| **Regional Expansion** | **Launch targeted promotions in the South region** (e.g., bundled offers, local advertising). | South accounts for only 8.5 % of revenue; a modest uplift can improve total sales without large additional spend. |
| **Sales Frequency** | **Implement a loyalty or repeat‑purchase program** to encourage more frequent orders. | Average order value is high, but order count is low; increasing order frequency can boost total revenue. |
| **Quarterly Monitoring** | **Set up a monthly dashboard** tracking revenue, profit, and margin by product and region. | Early detection of the current downward trend will allow timely corrective actions. |
| **Cross‑selling** | **Bundle Laptop with accessories (e.g., Chair, Table) at a slight discount** to raise average units per order. | Increases quantity sold per transaction and can improve margins on accessories. |
| **Supply Chain** | **Secure reliable suppliers for Laptop components** to avoid stockouts that could further depress sales. | Laptop is the top revenue driver; any supply disruption would have a large impact. |

Implementing these actions should help stabilize the declining sales trend, improve profit margins, and broaden the geographic footprint of the business.
